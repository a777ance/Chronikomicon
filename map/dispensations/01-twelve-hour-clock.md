## The core idea

12 chapters = 12 positions on a clock face. Not steps in a sequence. **Positions.**

The structure is **circular**, not linear. The end of the novel is adjacent to the beginning. What happens at position 11 touches position 12 which touches position 1. There is no true start, no true end — only where the reader enters.

The clock does not measure time. It measures **orientation** — where the novel stands in relation to itself.

---

## The pivots

| Position | Role | Quality |
|----------|------|---------|
| **12** | APEX | Highest point. Fullness. The moment before descent. |
| **6** | NADIR | Lowest point. Inversion. The moment before return. |
| **3** | LATERAL | Neither rising nor falling. A sideways pivot. |
| **9** | LATERAL | Neither rising nor falling. A sideways pivot. |

The four pivots are structural anchors. Everything between them is transition.

---

## Dispensations

Each position is governed by its own:
- **Internal logic** — what rules apply here; what is possible or impossible
- **Felt duration** — how long it *seems*, not how long it *is*
- **Governing quality** — the mood, texture, or pressure of this hour

One Dispensation can be vast. Another can be a sentence. The clock position is a character, not a length.
