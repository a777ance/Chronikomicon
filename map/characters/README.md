## The first unanswered question

**Who is this novel about?**

One person. Could be a name. Could be a description:
- *"An old man."*
- *"A woman who has stopped believing something."*
- *"A child who knows too much."*
- *"Me."*

This question has not been answered. It is the first question. Everything else waits for it.

---

## How to add a character

Create `map/characters/[name].md` with:
- Who they are (one sentence)
- Which dispensations they inhabit
- What they want
- What they cannot see about themselves
- Scripture anchor if relevant

Then link here and from relevant dispensation nodes.
