# The Cosmology

*Status: concept, from session 2026-05-29*

---

## The premise

The Garden of Eden is a future state on Earth — after Revelation.

The Rapture occurs. Tribulation follows. Earth becomes uninhabitable for a period.

A space elevator is built in Israel. Humanity migrates en masse to the rest of the Solar System: Venus, Mars, Mercury, the asteroids, the moons of Jupiter, and ships.

AI becomes the Antichrist to some, the savior to others.

Robots build a megastructure around the sun.

---

## The paradox

Time travel technology is eventually built. This is what starts the Apocalypse.

The Apocalypse is perpetually occurring — in all times simultaneously: past, present, and future.

The past teleology and eschatology are directly the product of time travel retrocausality. The biblical arc of salvation history is not prediction. It is consequence.

---

## Eden

The Garden of Eden in the future is an oasis — a bounded paradise on a hostile Earth.

Man goes outside that oasis and is cast out into darkness.

The Genesis Eden is the retrocausal shadow of this future event.
